About Us

Where curiosity comes first

Jupiter Kids Play School has been a trusted name in Madhepura since 2022, guiding children from their very first day of school through Class 8 with warmth, patience and purpose.

We believe learning should feel like play. Our classrooms are bright and hands-on, our teachers are trained in early-childhood care, and every child is encouraged to ask questions, explore, and be themselves.

  • Play Group, Nursery, KG and Classes 1–8
  • Activity-based, play-way learning approach
  • Trained and caring early-childhood educators
  • Indoor play area, art room and story corner
  • Safe transport with trained attendants
  • Small class sizes with individual attention

What is the minimum age for Play Group?

Play Group usually begins around age 2. Please confirm the exact cut-off with the school office.

How do I get the fee details?

Fees depend on the class. Please call or visit the school office, or send an enquiry through the form below.

What are the school timings?

Of course. Walk in during office hours, or call ahead to fix a time.
